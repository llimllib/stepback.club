### The Wagner-Bane connection

Through 10 games this season, Franz Wagner has as many assists to Desmond Bane for threes (10) as he did all of last season. He's assisted on nearly a third of Bane's threes (10 of 32), and Bane has changed his shot diet such that he's now shooting more than two-thirds of his shots from behind the arc.

### Green's reduced usage

Jalen Green's role in the Phoenix offense has declined precipitously, with his usage down to 22.5% from 30.9% last year. He's shooting more than 10 fewer shots per 100 possessions for a struggling Phoenix team.

### Curry with a star whistle?

Steph Curry is getting to the line more than he ever did before, which is powering his ultra-efficient season so far. He has a 72% true shooting percentage so far this season on 33% usage, which would be an all-time performance if he can keep it up

### The other Steph with a broken whistle

On the other side, Steph Castle can't get to the line at all, and it's affecting his efficiency: his field goal percentage near the rim is down from 64% to 44%. His assist rate is down from 11.6 per 100 possessions to 8.5 in San Antonio's new guard-heavy rotation; worth keeping an eye on how they manage that.

### Jaylen Brown finding his footing

Jaylen Brown's usage is down from 35% to 25%, and he also has the largest drop in fouls drawn in the NBA. Philadelphia's offense is struggling along with him, and he'll need to find a way to score efficiently to help them out.

### Wemby rim running

Victor Wembanyama is shooting 5 more shots per 100 at the rim than he did last season, and has increased his usage by more than 10% while keeping his efficiency steady.

### Board man gets paid

Ausar Thompson is repaying the Pistons for his new contract by nearly doubling his defensive rebound rate

### Draymond isn't shooting

Draymond Green is shooting the fewest shots per 100 possessions of any qualified player so far this season. He's assisting more this season and his turnover rate is up a bit, but it has the Golden State offense humming so far.
