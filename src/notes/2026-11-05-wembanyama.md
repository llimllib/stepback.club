Victor Wembanyama scored 52 against Dallas on Wednesday night, with 24
rebounds and 8 blocks, in a 112–111 Spurs win. A 50-point night happens
several times a season. What's strange about this one is the other end.

ESPN's [net points](https://espnanalytics.com/) split a player's game into
what they added on offense and on defense. Of the 220,676 player-games since
2018–19, Wembanyama's +12.5 on offense ranks 458th, top 0.2%. His
+8.4 on defense ranks 57th, top 0.03%. No other game is at least that
good on both. The best scoring nights, like Harden's 60 or Dončić's 73,
sit far to the right of the chart with about zero defense, and the best
defensive nights belong to big men who scored 9 or 17.
